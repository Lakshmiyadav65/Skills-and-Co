Refund and Cancellation Policy

When you can cancel, when you get your money back, and how to ask.

In short:

  • Cancel at least 48 hours before your batch starts: full refund.
  • Cancel within 7 days of your first class: refund minus the classes already held.
  • If we cancel your batch: a refund for the classes not delivered, or a free move to another batch.
  • Double charged or payment failed: the extra amount comes back in full.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for the live online courses offered by Skills and Co. ("we", "us" or "our"). It forms part of our Terms and Conditions. Nothing in it affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

2. How the course is delivered

Our courses are online services. Nothing is shipped. After your payment is confirmed, we send your joining details, batch schedule and class links to the email address or phone number you gave us, within 24 hours. Classes then run live on the days and times of your batch, in Indian Standard Time (IST). If you have not received your joining details within that time, please write to us via skillsandco.in.

3. If you cancel

  • If you cancel at least 48 hours before your batch's first class, we refund your full fee.
  • If you cancel within 7 days of your batch's first class, we refund your fee minus the value of the classes held up to the date you cancel, worked out pro rata over your plan.
  • After that, fees are not refundable, but you can ask to move to a later batch once, subject to seat availability.

4. If we cancel or change your batch

  • If we cancel your batch before it starts, you can choose a full refund or a free move to another batch. If we cannot continue running a batch once it has started, you can choose a refund for the classes not delivered or a free move to another batch.
  • If we cancel a single class, we will hold a replacement class. If we change your batch's timing and the new timing does not work for you, you can move to another batch or, if none suits you, ask for a refund of the classes not delivered.

5. When we do not refund

  • Classes you miss for your own reasons. See our Terms and Conditions for how to attend a missed class in another batch.
  • Problems with your own device, internet connection or availability.
  • Enrolments we end because of serious or repeated breaches of our class conduct rules.
  • Requests made after the refund window in section 3.

6. Failed, duplicate or wrong payments

If you are charged more than once for the same enrolment, or money leaves your account but the payment fails, the extra amount will be refunded in full. Failed payments are usually reversed automatically by the payment gateway or your bank within the timelines set by the Reserve Bank of India. If it has not reached you, write to us with the transaction reference and we will follow it up.

7. How to ask for a refund

Write to us via skillsandco.in with:

  • your full name and the email address or phone number you used to enrol;
  • your plan and batch;
  • the payment date and transaction reference, if you have it.

You do not have to give a reason, but it helps us improve. We will confirm your request and tell you the amount we will refund.

8. How and when you get your money

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show the money in your account. We cannot refund to a different account or payment method.

If you have a problem with a payment, please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank, so we can resolve it quickly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page on the date you paid applies to your enrolment.

10. Contact us

For any question about cancellations or refunds, write to us via skillsandco.in.
