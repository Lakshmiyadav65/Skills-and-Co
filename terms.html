2. The course

Our courses are live, trainer-led online classes in English speaking and communication. We currently offer two plans:

What each plan includes is what our website or your enrolment confirmation shows at the time you pay. We may improve or update course content over time, as long as the overall value of your plan is not reduced.

Your plan's access period starts on the first class of the batch you join, not on the date you pay. Student community access, including "lifetime" access in the 3 Months Mastery plan, lasts for as long as Skills and Co. runs that community.

5. Batches and schedule changes

Classes run in batches on fixed days and times. All timings are in Indian Standard Time (IST).

  • We may occasionally need to reschedule a class, change a batch's timing or have another qualified trainer take a session, for example because of public holidays, illness or technical problems. We will tell you at least 24 hours in advance where we can, and we will offer a replacement class for any class we cancel.
  • If we cannot run a batch at all, you can move to another batch or ask for a refund, as set out in our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
  • Classes you miss for your own reasons are not refunded or carried forward. Where seats allow, you may attend the same class in another running batch during your access period.
  • You may ask to move to a different batch once during your access period, free of charge, subject to seat availability.

6. Conduct in live classes

Our classes work because people feel safe to speak and make mistakes. In every class and in the student community, you agree to:

  • treat the trainer and other students with respect, and never harass, bully, threaten or discriminate against anyone, including on the basis of gender, caste, religion, region, language, accent or disability;
  • not share offensive, sexual, hateful or illegal content, or anything that infringes someone else's rights;
  • not disrupt a class, and follow the trainer's instructions on muting, camera use and turn-taking;
  • not advertise, sell or promote anything to other students, or collect their contact details for that purpose;
  • keep what other students share in class confidential.

If you break these rules, we may mute you, remove you from a class, or suspend or end your enrolment. If we end your enrolment for serious or repeated misconduct, we do not refund your fee.

Our classes are live and are not routinely recorded. If we record a session, for example for quality checks or for students who miss it, we will tell everyone at the start of that session. Any recording we share is for your personal use during your access period only.
